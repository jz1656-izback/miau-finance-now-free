## 📈 Market Terms

### Stock (a.k.a. "Mouse")
A tiny piece of a company you can buy. Like owning one square inch of a cat tree. Value goes up when the company does well, down when it spills the water bowl.

```
😸 "I own 10 shares of AAPL."
😿 "I mean... 10 mouses of AAPL."
```

### Ticker
A short code for a stock. Like a cat's name, but for companies.
- `AAPL` = Apple
- `TSLA` = Tesla
- `GME` = (No, not again.)

### Bull Market
When prices go up. Bulls charge forward. Cats love bull markets because more fish.

```
████████████████████░░░░  +85%
    "TO THE MOON" 🚀
```

### Bear Market
When prices go down. Bears hibernate. Cats nap through bear markets.

```
████░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░  -20%
    "Wake me when it's over" 😴
```

### Volatility
How much prices bounce around. High volatility = kitten on catnip. Low volatility = senior cat in sunbeam.

```
High vol:  📈📉📈📉📈📉  "ZOOMIES!"
Low vol:   📈📈📈📈📈📈  "Purrrr..."
```

### Liquidity
How easy it is to buy/sell without moving the price. High liquidity = wet food. Low liquidity = that one toy under the couch.

## 📊 Portfolio Terms

### Portfolio
Your collection of investments. Like a cat's stash of toys under the refrigerator.

### Diversification
"Don't put all your mice in one basket." Spreading money across different investments. A cat with 9 toys is safer than a cat with 1 toy.

### Asset Allocation
How you split your money between stocks, bonds, cash, crypto, etc. Think of it as dividing the food bowl into kibble, wet food, and treats.

### Rebalancing
Bringing your portfolio back to target weights. Like the cat redistributing toys after the human puts them all in one pile.

### Risk Tolerance
How much loss you can stomach before hissing. Low = senior cat. High = kitten with the zoomies.

## 📈 Analytics Terms

### Sharpe Ratio
Return per unit of risk. Higher = more fish per paw swipe.

```
Sharpe = Return / Risk
> 1.0 = Good hunter
< 0.5 = Lazy cat
< 0   = Sleeping on the job
```

### Alpha
Excess return above the market. The extra fish you caught beyond what any cat could catch.

### Beta
How much a stock moves with the market.
- Beta = 1.0: Moves exactly with the market (sheep cat)
- Beta > 1.0: Moves MORE than market (hyper cat)
- Beta < 1.0: Moves LESS than market (chill cat)

### Value at Risk (VaR)
"How much fish could I lose on a really bad day?"
- VaR 95%: "95% of days, I lose less than $X. But 5% of days..."
- Think: "I'm comfortable I won't lose more than $50 of toys... probably."

### Expected Shortfall (CVaR)
"What if the bad days are REALLY bad?" The average loss BEYOND VaR. The cost of when the cat knocks over the ENTIRE food container, not just one treat.

### Drawdown
Peak-to-trough decline. How far the portfolio fell from its high point. The distance from the top of the cat tree to the floor.

```
Peak:    $10,000  😸
Current: $7,500   😿
Drawdown: -25%    ← That's a long fall
```

## 🔮 Advanced Terms

### Monte Carlo Simulation
Running thousands of "what if" scenarios. Like simulating 10,000 cat lives to see where the portfolio ends up.

```
Simulation 1: Cat catches mouse → Portfolio +15%
Simulation 2: Cat gets distracted by laser → Portfolio -5%
Simulation 3: Cat naps all day → Portfolio flat
...
Simulation 10,000: Cat becomes president → Portfolio +∞
```

### Black-Litterman
A portfolio optimization that incorporates YOUR views. "I think tech will outperform. But not by too much." Black-Litterman balances your opinions with market consensus. A cat that knows its own hunting skills.

### Efficient Frontier
The set of portfolios with best return for a given risk. The curve of optimal fish-to-effort ratios.

```
Return ▲
       │    ╭──── Efficient Frontier
       │  ╭╯
       │ ╭╯  ← Optimal portfolios
       │╭╯    (Best fish per paw swipe)
       │╯
       └──────────────────▶ Risk
```

### Correlation
How two things move together.
- +1.0: Always move together (twin cats)
- 0.0: No relationship (cat + vacuum cleaner)
- -1.0: Always move opposite (cat + cucumber)

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)
Is a stock overbought or oversold? Like a cat's enthusiasm meter.
- RSI > 70: Overbought — "I'VE HAD ENOUGH CATNIP"
- RSI < 30: Oversold — "I haven't eaten in... 5 whole minutes"

### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
Two trend lines that signal momentum. Short-term cat mood vs long-term cat mood. When they cross, something happens.

## 💰 Economic Terms

### GDP
Gross Domestic Product. Total value of everything a country produces. Total kibble in the bowl.

### CPI
Consumer Price Index. How much more expensive things got. Inflation = fewer treats per dollar.

### Yield Curve
Interest rates for different time periods. Normal = long-term rates higher (healthy). Inverted = short-term rates higher (recession warning). Like a cat's tail: flat tail = danger.

```
Normal:    2yr: 3%  10yr: 5%  ↗  "Everything fine"
Flat:      2yr: 4%  10yr: 4%  →  "Hmm..."
Inverted:  2yr: 5%  10yr: 3%  ↘  "RUN! Recession incoming!"
```

### Fed Funds Rate
The interest rate the Federal Reserve sets. High rate = expensive borrowing = slow economy. Low rate = cheap borrowing = fast economy. The Fed controls the treat dispenser.

## 📄 Financial Statements (Simplified)

### Income Statement
How much money a company made. Revenue minus expenses = profit. In cat terms: mice caught minus naps taken = net mice.

### Balance Sheet
What the company owns vs owes. Assets = things you have. Liabilities = things you owe. Cat: owned toys vs toys still under negotiation with neighbor's cat.

### Cash Flow
Actual cash moving in and out. Different from profit! A company can be profitable but run out of cash. A cat can catch many mice but have no place to store them.

### 10-K / 10-Q / 8-K
SEC filings:
- **10-K**: Annual report. Yearly cat health checkup.
- **10-Q**: Quarterly report. Seasonal cat checkup.
- **8-K**: Material event. "CAT JUST DID SOMETHING IMPORTANT"

## V6 Purrantir MiauGlobe Terms

### Immortal Cat
The systemd user service (`immortal-cat.service`) that keeps all 8 standalone Vite frontends alive. Named because it resurrects crashed services every 15 seconds. Located at `/home/jevgeniz/Projekte/immortal-cat.sh`.

### Keplerian Position Engine
A simplified satellite orbit calculator in `backend/app/services/data/providers/satellite.py`. Computes real-time lat/lng for 17 orbital objects using inclination, period, phase, and longitude of ascending node. Used by the satellite layer on MiauGlobe.

### MiauGlobe
The WebGL-powered 3D globe at the heart of V6, built on `globe.gl`. Supports 11 data layers: companies, trade routes, cargo ships, mining, military bases, cats, aliens, satellites, night mode, terrain, and orbital paths.

### Data Provider
A Python class in `backend/app/services/data/providers/` that wraps one upstream data source or generates simulated data. Each follows the `DataSource` abstract base class with `fetch()`, `fetch_quote()`, and `_test_connection()`. V6 added 8 new providers: corporate, geopolitical, energy, conflict, satellite, alien, cargo, and mining.

### Orbital Path Arc
A curved line drawn on MiauGlobe showing a satellite's trajectory. Generated by computing multiple positions along the orbit elliptical via the Keplerian engine and rendering them as globe arcs.

### Spy Satellite Mode
A playful V6 feature that marks 6 classified reconnaissance satellites with 🕵️ markers. Toggled via the satellite layer controls on MiauGlobe. Data includes estimated launch date, operator (all "Classified"), and orbital parameters.

### x-files Easter Egg
A hidden keyboard easter egg in MiauGlobe. Type `x-files` or press `§` to unlock the alien UFO layer. Once unlocked, 25+ UFO sighting markers and ancient alien theory sites appear on the globe, with pink glow markers.

### Fortune Corporate Layer
A MiauGlobe data layer showing 42 Fortune Global HQ locations with company ticker, industry, revenue, and marker size scaled by market cap. Data served by the `corporate.py` provider. Click popup shows cat-ified details.
