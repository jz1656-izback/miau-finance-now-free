📖 Cat Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers that the cats bothered to type out

🔥 How do I report a portfolio fire?▾
Press the big red "🚨 Report an Emergency" button at the top. Or submit a ticket with category "Fire / Urgent". A cat firefighter will be dispatched within 2.4 purrs (approximately 47 seconds in human time). Do not attempt to extinguish the fire yourself. Cats are trained professionals.
🐱 What counts as a "fire"?▾
A "fire" is any urgent issue: your portfolio dropped 20%, the terminal won't start, the cat is not purring, or you accidentally deleted production. If you have to ask "is this a fire?", it probably is. When in doubt, set it on fire. Wait — don't. Call us.
⏱️ How fast do the cat firefighters respond?▾
Average response time is 2.4 purrs (≈47 seconds) for critical fires, 12 purrs (≈4 minutes) for high priority, and "when the cat feels like it" for everything else. Our cats are unionized and entitled to nap breaks every 47 minutes by law.
🐾 Can I adopt a firefighter cat?▾
No. These are highly trained professionals. They have families. They have tuna pensions. They have 401(k)s invested entirely in catnip futures. You cannot adopt them. However, you can sponsor a firefighter cat for just €4.99/month — you get a photo, a nameplate on their helmet, and a monthly purr report.
📋 How do I check my ticket status?▾
Go to the "Tickets" tab. Tickets move through three stages: "To The Rescue" (open), "On It!" (in progress), and "Extinguished" (resolved). You can also filter by category. If your ticket is stuck in "To The Rescue" for more than 24 hours, poke the cat. Literally — there's a "poke" button.
🐟 What if my question is about tuna?▾
All tuna-related inquiries are automatically escalated to Prof. Dr. Tuna (ESG & Sustainable Finance). He will respond with a 47-page dissertation on the tuna market outlook, a carbon pawprint analysis, and three recommendations for tuna futures. Expect his response within 2-3 business weeks. He is a fish. He has no hands. Be patient.
🚒 Do you fight real fires too?▾
The Miau Fire Brigade is certified to fight: portfolio fires, server fires, code review fires, and mild kitchen fires (toast level only). We do NOT fight: forest fires, relationship fires, or fires started by the cat playing with matches. The cat does not play with matches. The cat plays with your portfolio.
